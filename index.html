About the Therapy

What is myofunctional therapy?

Orofacial Myofunctional Disorders (OMDs) affect the muscles of the head and neck — with a special focus on the tongue and on nasal breathing. Myofunctional therapy is an exercise-based therapy that retrains those muscles. Most people practise the exercises for just five minutes, twice a day to get excellent results.

Breathing & airway

Nasal breathing, snoring, sleep-disordered breathing, UARS and obstructive sleep apnea — including support for people who struggle to tolerate CPAP.

Tongue, teeth & jaw

Tongue tie, tongue thrust, open bite, orthodontic relapse, TMJ discomfort and pre- and post-frenectomy or orthognathic surgery support.

The whole family

Children, teens and adults. Amy works alongside your dental and medical providers, using evidence-based, cutting-edge therapies and close collaboration.
