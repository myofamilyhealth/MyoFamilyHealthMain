About the Therapy

What is myofunctional therapy?

An exercise-based therapy that retrains the muscles of the mouth, face and throat. Most people practise just five minutes, twice a day.

Breathing & airway

Nasal breathing, snoring and sleep apnea — including support for people who struggle with CPAP.

Tongue, teeth & jaw

Tongue tie, tongue thrust, open bite, braces relapse and TMJ discomfort.

The whole family

Children, teens and adults. Amy works alongside your dental and medical providers.

Signs we look for

Mouth breathing Snoring Restless sleep Grinding teeth Tongue tie Tongue thrust Open bite Braces relapse Jaw & TMJ pain Picky eating Speech concerns Daytime focus trouble
